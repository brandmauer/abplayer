# abplayer

ABPlayer: the dream player tailored for audiobooks, music, and radio.

ABPlayer was created by someone who actually listens to music and audiobooks. There are no social networks, subscriptions, or recommendation algorithms here. The code contains no user accounts, analytics, or ads. It’s just you, the music, the audiobooks, the radio, and handy little features that were sorely missing from other audio players.

A–B Zoom. Mark the end of a segment with a single tap, and it zooms in or loops. This is perfect for tackling a tricky lecture paragraph or finding your place in files that are tens of hours long. An initial "micro-bookmark" appears automatically when playback starts, while an end marker is created when the sleep timer triggers or playback is paused. In "Zoom A ↔ B" mode, a dedicated slider appears just for that segment, allowing you to pinpoint the exact moment—even within a file lasting many hours.

A sleep timer that doesn't drive you crazy. You can set it for 15, 30, or 60 minutes, choose a custom duration, or select "until the end of the chapter" or "until the end of the playlist." A minute before stopping, the sound fades out smoothly and exponentially, like a closing door. If you haven't fallen asleep yet, just tap your phone twice, and the timer extends. Knock sensitivity is adjustable to prevent accidental triggers. Tapping the timer button adds a configurable amount of time. If the radio is playing, the timer closes the connection to the station.

Rewind for massive files. The rewind step is customizable. A single tap moves one step, while holding the button accelerates the rewind. Every track remembers where you stopped and resumes from that exact spot. Bookmarks save your exact position in long recordings.

Tailored sound. A ten-band equalizer plus separate controls for tone, speed, balance, and pre-amplification. There’s also "Precision Volume": physical buttons can be switched to fine-tune the level—handy for evening listening so the sound in your Bluetooth headphones is neither too quiet nor too loud, but exactly right. Equalizer profiles are linked to the output device: speaker, wired headphones, or Bluetooth devices.

Battery-friendly. Features include power-saving profiles, hardware audio decoding, and manual settings for network and background processes. Listening with the screen off extends your phone's battery life.

Plus, plenty of nice extras. Folder-based playlist grouping, presets for internet radio stations (audiobooks and music), cover art, favorites, shuffle, and multiple repeat modes. The track menu lets you view the codec, bitrate, and file path, with long-press actions to copy the details. Settings export and import make switching phones easy. Includes dark and light themes and customizable accent colors.

In short, ABPlayer is built for those who do more than just play audio in the background—it’s for serious listeners.
